# Diabetes Prediction & Bias Analysis

**Author:** Dr. Samuel Sunkanmi Goroti  
Lagos, Nigeria | gorotisunkanmi@gmail.com

---

### Overview
ML model predicting diabetes from clinical
features — with fairness audit across
demographic groups.

---

### Dataset
Pima Indians Diabetes — 768 patients, 8 features

---

### Key Findings
- Glucose = strongest predictor ✅
- Model accuracy peaks at 80% for ages 30-50
- Significant bias detected for under 30
  and over 50 age groups
- High false positive rate flagged —
  dangerous in low-trust health environments

---

### AI Safety Angle
Good average accuracy ≠ safe for all patients.
Age group bias means younger and older
patients — often most vulnerable —
are systematically underserved.
